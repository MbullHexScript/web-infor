# Admissions Management

Admissions content is published at `/id/pmb` and `/en/admissions`.

The page intentionally does not process applications internally. Official admission path names, dates, fees, quotas, and requirements must be entered from verified university sources.

Data sources:

- `pages` with `page_type=admissions`
- `admission_paths`
- `faqs` with `category=admissions`
- `site_settings` key `admissions.portal`

Privacy and accuracy rules:

- Do not fabricate fees, quotas, dates, or claims.
- Do not claim “best”, “number one”, or guaranteed employment without official evidence.
- Link to the official PMB portal only after verification.
